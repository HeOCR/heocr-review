Privacy policy

  • Sign in with Google is used only to verify your identity. From Google's token we use only the account's stable identifier (sub).
  • We do not store your email address, name or picture from your Google account.
  • Your reviewer id is a pseudonym: an HMAC-SHA256 of your Google identifier under a server secret key, truncated (for example rv_1a2b3c4d). Your Google account cannot be recovered from it.
  • A credit name is stored only if you choose one, with your consent and its date. You can remove it at any time in Settings.
  • One cookie: a signed sign-in session cookie (__Host-hr_session, 14 days). No analytics, no advertising, and no third-party scripts except Google's sign-in script. Google's privacy policy applies to the sign-in itself.
  • What we store (in a Cloudflare D1 database):
    • your pseudonym, when you joined and last visited, which terms version you accepted and when, and your credit name if you chose one;
    • a log of consents (terms accepted, credit name set or removed);
    • your decisions: the proposal, your decision, any text you edited, the date and the terms version;
    • corrections you have not yet confirmed or rejected on a page you are reviewing (the lines' text and marks), so they survive a reload or another device. They are deleted when you confirm or reject the page, and are never published.
    • proposals you skipped, and the proposal last shown to you (to spread work between volunteers);
    • rate-limit counters, keyed by your pseudonym or by a keyed hash of your IP address (never the address itself), deleted after about two days.
  • Contributions are public: your decisions and edited text are published in HeOCR datasets under CC0 1.0, credited to your pseudonym (or your credit name, if you chose one).
  • Check lines: some lines have a known answer. We compare your answers on them and keep an accuracy figure under your pseudonym. It is visible to admins only and never published. If it falls below the threshold, your decisions stop counting toward agreement between volunteers (they are kept, and you can count again).
  • Synthetic realism review stores your pseudonym, the image you chose, time to choice, rating, tags, and any optional comment. Only project admins can view and export these records for generator development.
  • Training page transcription stores your pseudonym, the text you enter, the line count, any count mismatch flag, and the time of saving. Project admins can access and export these records for training and line alignment.
  • For handwriting contributions we store your pseudonymous writer code linked to your sign-in pseudonym, your self-reported age band, your selected set, assigned pages and split. For writers under 18, we also store the guardian's sign-in pseudonym and their consent record. Consent and assent records identify the approved text by its version and digital fingerprint and include the date. We store a hash of the invitation code you used. We record the assigned page, file fingerprint, and time for each upload. We store uploaded photos and scans privately in Cloudflare R2. An approved contribution may be shared under CC0 1.0 after review. You or your guardian can withdraw in the app and use the withdrawal receipt to request deletion of uploads still in our custody. We retain the sheet assignment and split to avoid assigning them again. The operator keeps private roster snapshots and digest records for the enrollment audit. Once an approved contribution has been published under CC0 1.0, that dedication cannot be revoked and published copies cannot be recalled; withdrawing stops any further use of material not yet published and removes your enrollment record.
  • Deletion: email heocrproject@gmail.com with your pseudonym (shown in Settings). We delete your record and credit name from the site. For your decisions that are not yet published, you choose: we delete them, or we keep them with no link to you (no pseudonym and no name), so they can be published later as an anonymous contribution. If you don't choose, we keep them with no link to you. A decision already used in a registered evaluation set stays in any case, with no link to you, so published results can be reproduced. Decisions already published in public releases stay there, under your pseudonym only.
