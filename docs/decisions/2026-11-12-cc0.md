# 2026-11-12: contributions under CC0 1.0; the maintainer's own decisions relicensed to CC0

- **Decision:**
  1. The contribution terms change from CC BY-SA 4.0 to **CC0 1.0** for reviewers' decisions and edited text, before
     any volunteers join. New version `2026-11-12-v1` (terms and privacy policy); every reviewer is asked to accept
     it before their next decision.
  2. The maintainer releases **all of his own decisions and corrections in heocr-review under CC0 1.0, past and
     future**, including those made under terms `2026-11-07-v1`.
- **Approved by:** the maintainer, on 2026-11-12, relayed word for word by heocr-lead. Asked "Release your
  corrections as CC0, and change the app's terms to CC0 before any volunteers join?", he answered: "Yes, CC0 both".
- **Why:** HeOCR's adopted legal strategy publishes data under CC0. Eval references built from the maintainer's page
  reviews (`newprinted_midcentury_v0`) and line verifications should carry no share-alike condition.
- **Scope note:** CC0 covers a reviewer's own contribution only. Text a reviewer starts from keeps its own licence
  (Wikisource text, for example, stays CC BY-SA 4.0); the new terms say so.
- **Covers:**
  - the consent text in `src/shared/terms.ts` (`TERMS_HE`, `TERMS_EN`, `TERMS_LICENSE`, `TERMS_LICENSE_URL`), with
    `TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_VERSION` `2026-11-12-v1`;
  - `public/terms.html` (licence of contributions) and `public/privacy.html` (contributions are public), with the
    new version;
  - the export ledger's `contribution_licences` (the licence per terms version, and this relicence).
- **Effect:** decisions recorded under `2026-11-07-v1` stay as recorded, with their `terms_version` in the ledger.
  On 2026-11-12, before this change, production held 751 decisions from one reviewer, the maintainer (read-only
  count), so every decision recorded there is now CC0.
- **Not changed:** unsaved edits stay in memory only; the maintainer did not take up browser storage.
